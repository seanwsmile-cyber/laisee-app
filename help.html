使用說明

免費設計及下載 / Free design and download

應該上載咩相？ / Which photos work?

最多兩張清晰相片，盡量正面、光線充足、主體完整。支援 JPEG、PNG、WebP；如手機相片未能讀取，先轉成 JPEG。畫面會列出檔案限制及失敗原因。選擇「用 AI 製作」時，所選相片會一併送交 AI，但製作結果不保證完全重現所有特徵。

Choose up to two clear JPEG, PNG or WebP photos. Convert unsupported phone formats to JPEG. Selecting Create with AI sends all selected photos as references; generated likeness is not guaranteed.

「用 AI 製作」同「用原相製作」有咩分別？ / AI or original photos?

「用 AI 製作」會參考相片及款式繪製插畫；「用原相製作（不用 AI）」直接把相片放入款式，沒有 AI 重繪。結果頁會標示製作方式。AI 忙碌、超時或可用次數用完時，可按提示重試，或選擇用原相製作。

Create with AI redraws your photos as an illustration. Use Original Photos (No AI) places the photos into the design without AI redrawing. The result states which method was used. If AI is busy or unavailable, follow the retry instructions or use your original photos.

點解有使用次數限制？ / Why usage limits?

網站免費提供，但每次 AI 製作有成本。可用次數會於香港時間午夜重設，可按「更新狀態」查看。開始製作後會佔用次數，失敗或超時亦可能計次；繁忙時請稍後再試。

AI attempts cost money even though this tool is free. Limits reset at midnight Hong Kong time; select Refresh Status to check what is available. Each attempt uses your allowance, and failed or timed-out attempts may count too. If AI is busy, try again later.

可以修改完成咗嘅設計嗎？ / Can I edit my result?

展開「修改設計」，可修改相片、賀詞或填寫修改要求。修改設定後，預覽仍然係上一張成品；按「用 AI 製作」或「用 AI 再製作」，重新製作後先會套用修改，並使用一次 AI 額度。你亦可選擇「用原相製作（不用 AI）」。若製作失敗，上一張成品會保留，可繼續下載。

Open Edit Design to change photos, your greeting or revision notes. The preview keeps showing the previous artwork until you select Create with AI or Create Again with AI; this uses another AI attempt. You can also choose Use Original Photos (No AI). If creation fails, your previous artwork remains available to download.

下載邊個檔案？ / Which download?

一般按「下載利是封」，會下載已預留裁切邊位的正面 PNG：1125×2100 像素，內含 1050×2025 像素正面。按 300 dpi 排版時分別為 3.75×7 吋及 3.5×6.75 吋。像素尺寸不代表 AI 原圖一定有同等細節。下載前檢查人面、賀詞及邊緣；印前請由印廠確認尺寸、裁切邊位、色彩及刀模。

「其他下載格式」內的刀模展開圖只作示意，包含可見裁切／摺線，唔係經印廠確認嘅生產刀模。網站不提供下單或印刷服務。

Select Download Laisee for a front PNG with extra space for trimming: 1125×2100 pixels, including a 1050×2025 pixel front. At 300 dpi, these are 3.75×7 in and 3.5×6.75 in respectively. Check image quality and confirm dimensions, colour and trimming requirements with your printer. The die-cut layout under More Download Options is illustrative only, with visible guide lines. This site does not offer printing or ordering.

分享後朋友會見到咩？ / What gets shared?

「分享圖片」分享作品檔案；裝置不支援時，先下載再用通訊軟件傳送。「分享網站（不含作品）」只分享設計器網址，唔包含你嘅相片或作品。

Share Image sends the artwork file. If direct sharing is unavailable, download the image and send it using your messaging app. Share Website (Without Artwork) sends only the designer link, without your photos or result.

點樣保留作品？ / Keeping your result

重新整理或關閉頁面前，請先按「下載利是封」。網站冇雲端作品庫，本次設計不會在重新開啟頁面後還原。分享網站網址亦不會保存作品。

Select Download Laisee before refreshing or closing the page. There is no cloud gallery, so reopening the page will not restore your current design. Sharing the website link does not save your artwork.
